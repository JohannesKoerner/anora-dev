Security & Data

What happens to your data.

The essentials for your data protection and security review, in plain words. Binding are our Privacy Policy, the list of service providers and the Terms and Conditions.

At a glance

Servers in Germany

The web app, dashboard, API and database run on our servers at Hetzner in Germany.

Encrypted connections only

The website and SpecScout are only reachable over TLS; unencrypted requests are redirected.

Searches without personal data

A search holds product data. We store it without a user ID and without an IP address; only the server logs keep search terms with the IP address, for 30 days. Web search and AI models never learn who is searching.

No data processing agreement needed

SpecScout is meant for product data; we do not process personal data on your behalf. For the data of your user accounts, we are the controller ourselves (Terms and Conditions, section 11).

What happens to a search

What a search contains

Product name, brand, category, the specs you ask for and, if you set any, your source rules. Personal data does not belong in a search; the Terms and Conditions say so too.

Who is involved

Web search services receive search terms built from the product data. The pages found are fetched by our own servers. AI models receive the product name, brand, your spec definitions and the text of the pages. Some providers are based in the US or process data there, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the Standard Contractual Clauses. All service providers

How long we keep it

Inputs and results for 90 days, without a user ID. After that, only daily statistics without search content remain. For your allowance, we count your searches per month.

How your searches make SpecScout better

Evaluated pages teach SpecScout which sources are reliable, so it returns better search results. For this, we keep the address, text, the model's answer and the specs searched for of every page an AI model evaluates, for at most 12 months and without a user ID. We use them to check quality, compare models and train an algorithm that ranks sources. We do not train large language models with it. Your source rules feed into a shared rating of websites, without any link to your account.

How we protect your account

Accounts

  • Second factor through an authenticator app, with recovery codes
  • Export your data and delete your account yourself

API keys

  • Optional expiry date, revocable at any time
  • Access log per key, deleted after 30 days

Statutory retention duties, for example for invoices, remain unaffected. Details in the Privacy Policy, part C.
