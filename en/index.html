How SpecScout works

A simple workflow for fast, automated data enrichment

  1. 1

    Name the product

    Product name and brand, or an EAN. One by one in the web app, one request per product through the API.

  2. 2

    Choose the attributes

    You decide exactly which specifications to look for per category, and in which unit and format you need the results. Or simply pick and edit predefined specifications from our extensive library.

  3. 3

    Research and checks

    SpecScout reads manufacturer pages, datasheets (PDF) and reliable third-party sources. A value is only returned when two independent sources agree, or when an authoritative source such as the manufacturer confirms it.

  4. 4

    Use the result

    Every value comes with its sources and a confidence score, as a table in the web app or as JSON through the API. What couldn't be confirmed comes back as "not found".

What you get

Correct, and you can check it.

  • Every value is confirmed by at least two independent sources, or by an authoritative source such as the manufacturer.
  • Every value comes with its sources and a confidence score.
  • When a product comes in variants (say 256 and 512 GB), all values are returned with their sources instead of a guess.
  • Similar products are filtered out.
  • Values that can't be confirmed come back as "not found".
  • You decide which sites count: blacklist, trust or exclusively use sources for a brand, a category or both. More in the docs

Ready for your filters.

  • All values are normalised to the unit you want.
  • Ranges and dimensions as structured values.
  • Consistent specification definitions for every product in a category, so products can be compared reliably.
  • Optional fixed value lists per specification; synonyms and other languages are mapped onto those fixed values.

Hours of research, done in minutes.

  • About 17 seconds per product on average*, instead of searching shop pages and datasheets by hand.
  • Review instead of research: the confidence score and sources show at a glance which values you can trust and which you may want to double-check.
  • A specification library to start your categories from makes creating new categories quick and easy.
  • With Enterprise: a built-in AI assistant that drafts, adjusts and checks your specifications when you want to define your own.
  • With our batch search you can search for several products at once. While SpecScout is working, you can start the next search right away.

*measured in August 2026

When SpecScout is right for you, and when it isn't

A good fit if you

  • sell products with many technical attributes,
  • list or update many products regularly,
  • research product data by hand today, or leave fields empty because finding the data takes too long,
  • need structured, comparable data for filters and search.

Less of a fit if

  • your products have few technical attributes (fashion or home décor, for example),
  • your catalog is very small and rarely changes, so manual entry is manageable.

For developers

A REST API that fits into any system.

  • JSON over HTTPS; works with any system that can call a REST API, automation tools included.
  • Define your specifications once as a category; after that, product name, brand and category are all a search needs.
  • A search takes 10 to 40 seconds; run it as a background job with a timeout of at least 180 seconds.
  • Up to 200 requests a minute per key; only completed searches count against your quota.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SpecScout cost?
Free access gives you 20 searches a month. For more searches, the AI assistant for spec definitions and personal support, there is Enterprise, with the price on request. See pricing.
Can I test the data quality?
Of course. You can test SpecScout with 20 free searches a month: Start free. If you need more searches for your evaluation, contact us.
Where does the data come from?
The data comes from sources on the web, such as manufacturer pages, datasheets (PDF) and online shops. Every value needs at least two independent sources or one authoritative source such as the manufacturer, and comes with links to its sources.
How does SpecScout make sure the values are correct?
SpecScout compares technical specifications from several sources. A value is only returned when at least two independent sources agree, or when an authoritative source such as the manufacturer confirms it; otherwise the attribute is listed as "not found". Pages about a different model (for example the S24 Ultra when you search for the S24) are rejected. Every value comes with links to its sources, so you can check it.
SpecScout uses AI. Couldn't this lead to hallucinations?
AI can make mistakes, so SpecScout never relies on a single AI answer. A value is only returned when at least two independent sources agree, or when an authoritative source such as the manufacturer confirms it, and pages about a different model are rejected. Values that can't be confirmed come back as "not found" instead of a guess, and fixed value lists restrict the AI to the values you allow. Every value comes with links to its sources, so you can check it.
How do I connect SpecScout to my system?
SpecScout is a web app and an API. In the web app, you search one product at a time. Through the REST interface, you retrieve the data as JSON, so SpecScout works with any system that can call a REST API, including automation tools such as Zapier, n8n or Microsoft Power Platform. The details are in the API documentation.
Is SpecScout suitable for complete automation?
Partly. SpecScout does the research completely on its own. We do recommend, however, that you check the data to an appropriate extent before you use it. SpecScout makes this easy, because every value comes with links to its sources and a confidence score. Check above all values with a low confidence score, particularly critical information, and attributes with several variants.
Is my data stored or shared?

To process your request, product names, the attributes you're looking for and the web pages found are forwarded to service providers for web search and AI models; they are named in our list of service providers. We store the search data (your input, the values found and their sources) without reference to your account and condense it into daily statistics after 90 days. We also keep samples from search processes (e.g. web pages and AI answers) for up to 12 months for caching (faster answers to future searches), quality assurance and analyses to improve SpecScout. This data stays with us and is not shared with other third parties.

Details are in our terms (clause 7) and our privacy policy.
