Transparent

Your customers want to know exactly what they are buying. "Runs smoothly, large and crisp display" is vague and won't convince anyone who knows what they need. With SpecScout, the technical data is just one click away.

Filterable

Nowhere does it say exactly how large the screen in the upper listingon the left is. Anyone filtering your shop by screen size or HDMI won't find the laptop. SpecScout delivers the attributes you define, in your format, so every device has the same fields, new or used.

As sellable as new

When buyers are looking for a product, used and new products compete. The new one often wins because it is easier to check that it has the right specs. If your listing is as well documented as a new product, condition and price decide. And on price, the used device comes out ahead.

How SpecScout fits your intake

  1. 1

    Intake and grading

    You check and grade the device as you do today. Condition, battery health and grading stay with you.

  2. 2

    Add the model's data

    Model name and brand, or an EAN, is enough: SpecScout returns the attributes you define, in your units and value lists.

  3. 3

    Create the listing

    Complete, filterable attributes for your shop and your marketplace listings. Automated and scalable for your entire inventory through the API.
