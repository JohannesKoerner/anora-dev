About us

Two travelers, a sailing boat and the challenge of finding a laptop.

How SpecScout started, and who is behind it

Our story

Our founders' story starts on a sailing boat, shortly after they finished high school. Erik and Johannes both had ambitious plans. Erik would go on to spend two years traveling South America, Johannes would hitchhike around the entire globe. At the beginning of their journey, they met one of their biggest challenges on the coast of the Atlantic: crossing it. Without an airplane, because that would have been too easy. Both ended up in Gibraltar, where they found work on the same boat that would get them to the Canary Islands. That's how their story started. There, they split up again and took different boats on the remaining leg to America.

Erik went on traveling through South America afterwards, while Johannes continued his trip around the world. The two never lost touch, though. Erik then started his first startup with another friend.

Life went on, until one day Johannes tried to buy a used laptop online. He felt incredibly frustrated by how hard it was to find used laptops that fit his requirements. Johannes started thinking about the issue and coming up with ideas on how to solve it.

When it was clear that help was needed, Johannes called Erik, who brought along the experience from his first startup. The two decided to start Anora Systems. In 2025, they started working on SpecScout full time.

“We're living in the 21st century. It can't be that it's still this hard to find used products online that fit your needs.”

Johannes Körner, Co-Founder

That became SpecScout: complete, filterable product data for every listing, so customers find what fits them. For new products as much as for used ones.
